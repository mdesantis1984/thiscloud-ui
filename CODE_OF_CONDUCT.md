# Code of Conduct

[Español](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.es.md) · [Documentation](docs/README.en.md)

This Code of Conduct covers participation in this repository's issues, pull requests, and discussions. It does not promise a reporting service.

## Working together

- Be respectful and constructive. Discuss work and decisions without attacking people.
- Welcome different experience levels and perspectives; offer feedback about the contribution, not the contributor.
- Do not harass, threaten, use discriminatory language, or repeatedly disrupt a discussion.
- Do not publish someone's personal information, private messages, or incident evidence without their permission.

## Requesting a private contact route

If you need to raise a conduct concern and **Request for private contact / Solicitud de contacto privado** is visible in the [repository issue chooser](https://github.com/mdesantis1984/thiscloud-ui/issues/new/choose), you may use it to request private contact by confirming its two required checkboxes. This creates a **public issue**, not a private report. Its title and comments remain editable; the form cannot prevent sensitive submissions. Do not add incident descriptions, names, screenshots, evidence, private messages, or contact details anywhere in the issue. A request does not establish a private channel or guarantee a reply. Only share relevant information if a suitable private channel is independently agreed. If the form or a private channel is unavailable, do not use another public form or post incident details.

For violations of GitHub's platform rules, use [GitHub Report Abuse](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse) separately from repository conduct review. For vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not the conduct-contact form. Neither path is a substitute for a private conduct channel.

## Repository moderation

The repository owner named in [CODEOWNERS](.github/CODEOWNERS) may review conduct affecting repository participation, request a change of tone, hide or remove content, close a discussion, or restrict participation where the platform permits. These are possible responses, not an automatic sequence or a guaranteed outcome. No response time, confidentiality guarantee, or independent appeal panel is offered.
